Firebase Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how users interact with Mia Princess. Firebase Analytics may automatically collect app instance identifiers, device and app information, approximate location information, session information, screens viewed, app interactions, engagement information, and events configured by us.

We use this analytics information to understand feature usage, measure user engagement, identify technical or gameplay issues, and improve the App. Advertising identifiers may be collected or used only where made available by the operating system and permitted by the App’s configuration, platform requirements, and the user’s applicable privacy choices.

We also use Firebase Crashlytics to identify, analyze, and resolve crashes, errors, application-not-responding events, and other stability problems. Crashlytics may collect Crashlytics installation UUIDs, Firebase installation identifiers, crash traces, stack traces, exception information, app version, operating system and device information, timestamps, application state, and related diagnostic information.

When Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are used together, Analytics events and screen information may be included as breadcrumb information in crash reports to help us understand the actions that occurred before a crash.

We do not intentionally submit names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment card information, or other directly identifying information to Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics through custom events, user identifiers, logs, or custom diagnostic fields.

Local Data

When the App or its third-party SDKs operate, technical and usage information may be collected automatically, including device model, operating system version, app version, language, region, network status, installation or app instance identifiers, advertising identifiers where available, diagnostics, crash information, performance data, app interactions, advertising interactions, and timestamps.

For data directly controlled by us, we retain technical logs and usage information only for as long as reasonably necessary for troubleshooting, service stability, analytics, fraud prevention, and legal compliance, and generally no longer than 12 months.

Google Analytics event-level data is generally retained for 2 months. User-level data associated with pseudonymous identifiers is generally retained for 14 months after the user’s most recent activity, because the retention period is reset when new user activity occurs. Aggregated reporting data may be retained for a longer period where it no longer relates to a particular user or app instance.

According to Google’s current Firebase documentation, Firebase Crashlytics generally retains crash stack traces and associated installation identifiers for 90 days before beginning the removal process. Information processed by other third-party SDK providers is retained according to their respective privacy policies.
